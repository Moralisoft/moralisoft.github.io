Sudoku Clarity Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Sudoku Clarity is made by Moralisoft ("we"). The app is designed to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your saved games, settings, statistics and game history are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We (the developer) never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Optional rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

Free players can choose to watch a short video to earn an extra hint. Videos are shown only when you tap to watch one; there are no banner or pop-up ads. Videos are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (for example the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice later. Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Pro players never load or see ads.

In-app purchase (Google Play)

The one-time "Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your Google account.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you open the leaderboards, the app uses Google Play Games to sign you in with your Play Games profile and to submit your best solve times and longest daily streak. Your Play Games name and avatar are shown next to your scores, according to your Play Games privacy settings. Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can change or delete your Play Games profile in your Android settings (Google → Play Games).

No accounts, no analytics

The app itself has no accounts (leaderboards use your optional Play Games profile) and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs of our own.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load optional videos, verify purchases and update leaderboards.
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.
